01

Predicting Student Final Exam Scores

An end-to-end regression pipeline built on an 800-student dataset to uncover which habits best predict exam performance — designed to flag at-risk students before the exam, not after.

  • Reduced average prediction error to 2.47 points (MAE), RMSE 3.35
  • Nearly doubled explanatory power: single-feature baseline (R² 0.22) → full 6-feature model (R² 0.40)

Key insight: sleep hours predicted final scores as strongly as study hours — a pattern that wouldn't have surfaced from intuition alone.

  • Used an 80/20 train-test split to check performance on unseen records.
  • Engineered six features and compared them against a single-feature baseline.
  • Reviewed residuals to identify students the model under-predicted, flagging them for manual review rather than trusting the average error blindly.

02

COVID-19 Global Data Analysis

A country-level analysis of roughly 49,000 records across 187 countries, tracking spread, mortality, recovery, and data reliability — 654K deaths and a 3.97% global death rate at the latest snapshot.

  • Built a custom weighted impact score (40% cases, 40% deaths, 20% inverse recovery)
  • Compared death and recovery rates, not just raw case totals, across countries

Key insight: the UK, Belgium, and Italy had the highest death rates (11–15%) despite not leading in total cases — Qatar and Turkey topped recovery rates instead.

  • Compared rates rather than raw case totals to make country comparisons meaningful.
  • Found a strong 0.93 correlation between confirmed cases and deaths.
  • Flagged inconsistent recovered-case reporting across countries (r = 0.68 with active cases) — a data-quality signal, not just a stat.

04

Medical Insurance Charges Predictor

An end-to-end regression app trained on 1,339 patient records, comparing four models (Ridge, RandomForest, GradientBoosting, XGBoost) via GridSearchCV to predict annual insurance charges, deployed as a live Streamlit tool.

  • Selected the best model by cross-validated RMSE — R² 0.875, MAE $2,003 on the test set
  • Built a shared feature-engineering module so training and the deployed app can never drift apart

Key insight: smoking status combined with high BMI and older age forms a distinct, much higher-cost group — a stronger signal than any single factor alone.

  • Engineered 13 additional features (interactions, log transforms, risk flags) on top of the 6 raw inputs.
  • Ranked models by cross-validated RMSE, not test RMSE, to avoid leaking the test set into model selection.
  • Shipped a feature-version guard so the app fails loudly instead of predicting on mismatched columns if the model goes stale.
